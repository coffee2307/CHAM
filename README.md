# CHẠM Shopify Theme

Storefront foundation for CHẠM — a Vietnamese craft marketplace focused on accessible curated products, clear provenance, and product-specific custom orders.

## Direction
- Warm contemporary Vietnamese craft palette: ivory, clay, terracotta, saffron and apricot.
- Multi-product marketplace structure with standard Shopify purchasing.
- Text wordmark by default; brand assets can be supplied later through Shopify.
- Homepage includes search, categories, featured products, trust/value explanation, and a custom-order entry.
- Product pages use standard Shopify purchasing with an additional CHẠM provenance/custom layer.

## Product metafields prepared
Namespace: `cham`
- `origin`
- `material`
- `craft`

Products tagged `custom`, `Custom`, or `tuy-chinh` receive a custom badge on CHẠM featured cards.
